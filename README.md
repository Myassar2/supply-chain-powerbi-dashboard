# Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** built using the **DataCo Global Supply Chain dataset** to analyze business performance across sales, logistics, and customer behavior.

The goal is to transform raw data into **actionable insights** that support better decision-making in supply chain operations.

---

##  Objectives
- Identify the most profitable products and categories  
- Analyze shipping performance and delivery delays  
- Compare revenue and cost across regions  
- Understand customer distribution and behavior  
- Track key performance indicators (KPIs)  

---

##  Dashboard Features

###  Key KPIs
- Total Profit: **3.97M**
- Total Revenue: **33M**
- Total Orders: **180K**
- Average Shipping Time: **3.5 days**
- Late Delivery Rate: **55%**

---

###  Analysis Included
- Profit by category  
- Revenue vs Cost by region  
- Delivery performance (planned vs actual)  
- Top products and departments  
- Customer segmentation and payment methods  
- Order trends over time  
- Geographic distribution of customers  

---

##  Key Insights

- A small number of categories (such as *Fishing* and *Cleats*) contribute disproportionately to total profit, indicating dependency on top-performing segments and a potential diversification opportunity.  

- Standard and Second Class shipping modes show longer delivery times, suggesting a need to optimize logistics processes or shift demand toward faster shipping options.  

- Order demand is concentrated in a limited number of regions, particularly in North America and parts of Europe, indicating geographically focused business operations.  

- A small group of products contributes significantly to total orders, highlighting strong customer preferences and potential reliance on best-selling items.  

- Order volume shows a declining trend over time, which may indicate decreasing demand or operational inefficiencies that require further investigation.  

- Technology and Footwear departments achieve higher average profit per order, suggesting stronger profitability compared to other departments.
